# Attendee guide

You do not need an account or an app, and there is nothing to fill in. Scan the organiser's QR code with your camera,
or type the event's **room code** into the six boxes on the home page (it is shown as two triplets, like `ABC-123`).
Both open the same page.

![The home page with the join-by-code boxes](/screens/landing.png){width=280}

## You are in as soon as the page opens

Opening the page joins you. Qroove gives you a **codename** of two words, a vegetable and a sea animal, such as
_Carrot Octopus_. It is shown at the top of the page. If you need the organiser's help, tell them your codename:
it is what they see beside your songs. Other guests do not see it. Your phone keeps a private pass for this event
only, so coming back to the page keeps your codename and your songs.

## The event page

![The event page for a guest](/screens/attendee-event.png){width=280}

Everything is on one screen. Only the queue scrolls.

- **Status pill (top right):** _Live_, _Paused_, _On a break_, _Not started_ or _Ended_; _Reconnecting_ or _Offline_
  when your signal is the problem.
- **Now playing:** the current song and its genre.
- **The queue:** every song still to come, in the order it will play. Yours have a coloured edge and say **Yours**,
  with `~N min` when the queue can predict it. Songs that would run past the planned end are greyed and labelled
  _After end time_; they may still play if the organiser extends the time. Songs the organiser pre-loaded give way
  to requests, so yours moves ahead of them.
- **＋ Add a song**, with how many of your songs you have used (for example _3 of 20_).

## Adding music: paste, then one tap

1. Tap **＋ Add a song**.
2. Search Spotify in the box and tap a song, or paste a link to a song or a playlist (Spotify, YouTube Music,
   YouTube, Apple Music). **Paste** reads your clipboard only when you tap it.
3. Tap **Add as Salsa** or **Add as Bachata** (whichever genres the event plays). That adds the songs and closes
   the form.

With a playlist you can sort before adding: drag a song by its grip (⠿), swipe left to remove it, swipe right to
send it to the top. Only as many songs as you have left are added; the rest are greyed out below a dashed line.
Songs already in the event are skipped and do not use up your limit. If you ask for a song the organiser pre-loaded,
it simply becomes yours.

## Taking a song back

In the queue, **swipe your song to the left**: a red bin appears, and letting go past a third of the way withdraws
it. Or tap **✕** on the row and then **Remove**. This frees the slot straight away. A song that is playing or has
played cannot be withdrawn.

If the organiser removes one of your songs, or a link could not be found, a line under the queue says so.

## Light and dark

The button at the top right switches between dark (default, for dim rooms) and light.
